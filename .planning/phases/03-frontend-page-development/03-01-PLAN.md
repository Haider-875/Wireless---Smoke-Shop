# PLAN 03-01: Frontend Page Development

## Objective
Recreate the reference website's primary target page (`/repairs`) with 100% visual and structural fidelity based on the reverse-engineered components:
1. Hero Section with `.glow-gold` ambient lighting, fluid typography, direct call button, and walk-in address link.
2. "What We Fix" Service Grid: 6 service cards with icon badges, hover border transitions, and price disclaimer.
3. 3-Step Process Grid: Background `#08080a`, tabular numbered steps (`01`, `02`, `03`).
4. "Bring it by the counter" CTA Banner: Rounded-3xl card with store hours, clock icon, and direct call trigger.
5. Storefront Home View (`/`): Store overview matching the luxury aesthetic.

## Tasks
1. **Refine Service Cards & CSS**: Add exact `.bugs-service-card`, `.bugs-cta-card`, and `.step-card` styles in `bugs-theme.css`.
2. **Recreate `/repairs` View**: Update `resources/views/pages/repairs.blade.php` to match the exact HTML hierarchy, typography, buttons, and card layouts of the reference.
3. **Recreate `/` Home View**: Update `resources/views/pages/home.blade.php` with high-fidelity hero and highlights.
4. **Automated Feature Verification**: Update `tests/Feature/RoutesAndLayoutTest.php` to verify all sections, cards, and CTA copy on `/repairs`.
5. **Phase 3 Summary & Execution Report**: Document deliverables and verify strict absence of Tailwind CSS.
